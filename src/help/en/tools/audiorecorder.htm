Audio Recorder

The Audio Recorder lets you record the audio signal present at the microphone or line-in input and save it to a sound file. Clicking the Start button begins the recording. Stop ends the recording. If you forget to do this, the recording is stopped automatically after 120 minutes.

After the recording has ended, you can listen to it (Playback) or save it to a file. When saving, GZIP compression is also supported if the file name ends in *.gz.

Caution! You may first need to turn up the microphone or line-in input in your computer's operating system so that the corresponding audio signals can be recorded. Whether this is the case, and whether you have connected the audio source to the correct jack on your computer, can be seen from the level meter contained in the Audio Recorder. This becomes active once you click Start.

Options

Before you start recording, you can specify the device and the quality (sample rate, resolution, mono/stereo) to record with. If the sample rate is set to Standard, the recorder attempts to record at CD quality (44.1 kHz).

The mono/stereo selection defaults to mono, because modern notebooks and laptops increasingly have only a mono microphone jack as an input, and recording is therefore sometimes only possible in mono.
