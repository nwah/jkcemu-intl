K1520 Sound Card

For computers with a K1520 bus connector, and thus also for some of the computers emulated by JKCEMU, a sound card was developed that uses the AY-3-8910 or AY-3-8912 chip. A CTC is also included.

The sound card can be flexibly configured via jumpers. In JKCEMU, this card is emulated with the following settings:

Clock frequency of the sound chip: 1.79 MHz
Clock frequency of the CTC: = clock frequency of the emulated system
CTC input channel 0: = one quarter of the clock frequency of the sound chip, i.e. 447.5 kHz
CTC input channel 1: CTC output channel 0
CTC input channel 2: CTC output channel 1
CTC input channel 3: CTC output channel 2
Assignment of sound channels: A: Left, B: Center, C: Right
I/O addresses: 38h: select or read sound register
39h: write sound register
3Ch: CTC channel 0
3Dh: CTC channel 1
3Eh: CTC channel 2
3Fh: CTC channel 3

Caution! In NANOS, the sound card is operated in the different I/O address range E0h to E7h.
