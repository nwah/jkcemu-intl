High-Resolution Screens

On high-resolution screens, i.e. screens with a resolution of more than 75 dpi (Dots Per Inch), the JKCEMU windows sometimes look rather small. To solve this problem, JKCEMU offers two options:

The first option is to simply let the JKCEMU windows be scaled larger. This requires Java 9 or a higher Java version. On some operating systems, however, scaling only works in 100% steps.

Scaling can sometimes look a bit unattractive when the objects being scaled have hard edges. This is the case with the screen output of the emulated systems as well as with the icons. For this reason JKCEMU offers another option: you can freely choose the fonts and font sizes and switch between two sizes for the icons.

Whether and which option you choose depends on your circumstances and your taste. You will find both options in the Settings.
